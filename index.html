Have a question?

Here’s what to know about Lina Criativa’s Christmas Pack.

What do I receive with my purchase?

You receive English-language digital files for your chosen pack. Basic includes only the Mini Bible verse booklet, with 20 inspiring Bible verses in each booklet. Complete includes the Mini Bible, notepad, mini calendar, packaging, display cards, and the other projects shown on this page.

What’s the difference between the two packs?

Basic is $5.90 and includes only the Mini Bible verse booklet. The Complete Christmas Pack is $14.90 and includes every design in the catalog, including the notepad. The difference between the regular pack prices is $9.

Will finished gifts be shipped to my home?

No. This is a digital download. You print the designs and assemble the gifts. Paper, printing, ribbons, accessories, treats, mugs, and other physical items in the photos are not included.

What file formats are included?

Your English-language files come in PDF, PNG, and Silhouette formats. Choose the format that works for your production process.

Are the files editable? Can I add a name?

The designs are ready to print, and you can add your customer’s name. They are not fully editable templates for changing colors, illustrations, or layouts.

Do I need a Silhouette cutting machine?

The pack includes PDF and PNG files as well as Silhouette files. Cutting and assembly depend on the piece and format you choose. Mug designs require a separate production process and suitable equipment.

Can I sell the gifts I make?

Yes. You may use the files to produce and sell physical gifts. You may also make gifts for personal use. You may not resell, share, distribute, or transfer the digital files.

Can I combine the pieces into gift sets?

Yes. The collection includes coordinating designs, so you can give a Mini Bible verse booklet on its own, pair it with a notepad, or put together a matching gift set using the packaging in the Complete Pack.

Do the photos show designs from the pack?

The photos and mockups show designs from the collection and how they can look when assembled. Finishing materials and physical items are shown as presentation ideas and are not included with the files.

Is the mini calendar up to date?

Yes. The included calendar pages are updated for 2027.

How do I receive the files after purchase?

Once your payment is approved, you’ll receive access instructions at the email address you used at checkout. Follow those instructions to download your pack.

Who is this pack for?

It’s for people who enjoy making Christmas gifts, paper crafters, and handmade business owners. Make pieces for friends and family or sell your finished gifts. You’ll need suitable materials and equipment for printing, cutting, and assembling each project.

Does the pack include a guarantee?

Yes. You have 7 days from your purchase to explore the files and request a refund if the pack isn’t what you expected. The guarantee applies to both packs.
